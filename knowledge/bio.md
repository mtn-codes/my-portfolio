## Extended Bio

Taha Naghdi is an Iranian filmmaker, digital content strategist, and software engineer with a multidisciplinary track record. His core background began in cinematic videography, advanced film editing, dramatic lighting, and motion graphics, which naturally expanded into AI-driven content generation and digital strategy.

As Founder and Manager of Tirazh Advertising Agency, he has led nationwide advertising campaigns encompassing cinematic video commercials, specialized design & printing, and bespoke software solutions. Through executive and directorial collaborations, he has created industrial documentaries for major enterprises (including Saina Textile Factory, Simfam Aluminum Factory, Black Tiara Stone Factory, and Favania Construction), high-end commercial campaigns for retail brands (Plaque 8 Groom Salon, Ebrahimi Watch Gallery, Masirino Health, and Enzo Furniture), and real-motion cultural productions for the Ministry of Culture & Islamic Guidance (Ershad).

Parallel to his filmmaking, Taha is an active software developer and automation builder. He develops modern web applications using React, Node.js, and PostgreSQL, builds AI agents, and prototypes smart gadgets using Python and microcontrollers. He completed the Harvard CS50 Summer Course, holds a Cinematography Certificate from Ghermez Cinema Academy, an IoT & Circuit Design Certificate from TVTO, a Finland University AI Certificate, and is currently advancing his BSc in Computer Engineering and MBA.
